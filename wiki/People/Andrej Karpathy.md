# Andrej Karpathy

Founding member of OpenAI, former director of AI at Tesla. Prolific educator (Zero-to-Hero series, nanoGPT). Originator of the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] framing — the idea that an LLM curates a markdown wiki rather than running RAG over raw docs.

Referenced (not present) in [[2026-05-08-ai-hackathon-show-and-tell]] (~24:39): "Karpathy is — I don't know, maybe an AI influencer or probably a guy. The stuff he's coming out with is incredible. The auto research is incredible and then the idea of a wiki — he just had this little shower thought to him. The wiki is a really good format that humans love and the bots are also really good at reading them."

[[Robb Green]] (~25:44) confirms: "I use it as my base memory now is the LLM wiki by Karpathy."

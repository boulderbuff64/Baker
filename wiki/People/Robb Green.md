# Robb Green

Architect of the [[Command Centre]] — a unified platform for autonomous knowledge-compounding agents. Heavyweight presenter on the [[2026-05-08-ai-hackathon-show-and-tell]] call (~28-34 min architecture walkthrough; ~1:07-1:14 Enigma Engine demo).

Operates a small team running agents at scale: 50,000 slideshows analyzed/posted per day, with an in-development Meta ads counterpart ([[Enigma Engine]]). Coined "context farming" ([[Farmer Pattern]]) and the [[Intel Channel]] team-distribution pattern.

Style: model-agnostic ("I built everything to be swappable"), cloud-first ("everything's in the cloud, even if I'm on a plane"), framework-driven ("I'm a big believer in models and frameworks"). Names his agents — Open Claw instance is "Jarvis," uses ⚡ [[Lightning Bolt Acknowledgment]] in Telegram.

Uses Pinecone (vector memory) + Supabase (state) + Claude Code (runtime). Recently switched/switching from Open Claw to Hermes for the built-in self-learning loop.
